# E-Commerce Revenue & Customer Analytics

## Project Overview

This portfolio project investigates historical e-commerce sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, delivery efficiency, and economic relationships using real-world transaction data. The project simulates an analytical engagement for MercadoVista Commerce Analytics, a fictional Brazilian e-commerce marketplace. The underlying data comes from the public Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Dataset.

**Role:** Data Analyst / Economic Research Analyst

**Tools:** Microsoft Excel, MySQL, Python, Tableau, R Programming

**Status:** In-Progress

## Business Problem

Marketplace leadership needs a better understanding of revenue performance, customer retention, and operational efficiency. My objective is to transform historical transaction data into actionable business insights that can support strategic decisions.

## Project Objectives

1. Evaluate historical sales performance and revenue trends.
2. Analyze customer purchasing patterns and repeat purchasing.
3. Investigate delivery performance and customer satisfaction.
4. Examine economic relationships between freight charges, spending, and business outcomes.
5. Develop evidence-based business recommendations.

## Research Questions

- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- How does revenue change over time?
- What proportion of customers make repeat purchases?
- How do customer purchasing patterns vary geographically?
- Are delivery delays associated with lower customer satisfaction?
- How do freight charges relate to customer spending?

## Data Source

**Dataset:** Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist

**Source:** https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce

**Historical coverage:** 2016–2018

The dataset includes orders, order items, customers, products, sellers, payments, reviews, and delivery information.

## Analytical Tools

- **Excel:** KPI calculations, PivotTables, scenario analysis, and dashboards.
- **MySQL:** Relational database design, SQL queries, customer cohorts, and RFM segmentation.
- **Python:** Data cleaning, exploratory analysis, and revenue forecasting.
- **R:** Statistical hypothesis testing, regression analysis, and economic interpretation.
- **Tableau:** Interactive executive dashboards and geographic analysis.

## Methodology

1. Business understanding and data validation
2. Excel exploratory business analysis
3. MySQL database development
4. Python customer analytics and forecasting
5. R statistical and economic analysis
6. Tableau visualization and executive recommendations

## Limitations

This is a historical observational dataset. Findings do not automatically establish causation.

The dataset does not provide complete product cost, advertising expense, or price-experiment information. Therefore, actual profitability, marketing ROI, and causal price elasticity cannot be reliably estimated without additional data.
