# Project Preview: E-Commerce Revenue & Customer Analytics

## 1. Business Context

MercadoGente Commerce Analytics is a fictional Brazilian marketplace seeking to understand its historical commercial and operational performance. Their analysis will use public Olist transaction records.

## 2. Primary Objective

Identify measurable opportunities to improve revenue performance, customer engagement, and delivery-related customer experience.

## 3. Preview

**Included:**
- Historical merchandise sales analysis
- Product category and geographic performance
- Customer purchasing frequency and segmentation
- Delivery delays and review scores
- Statistical analysis of selected relationships
- Revenue forecasting and hypothetical scenarios

**Excluded:**
- Actual net profit estimation
- Causal price elasticity estimation
- Advertising return on investment
- Real-time operational monitoring

## 4. Initial KPIs

1. Product revenue
2. Total orders
3. Average order value
4. Monthly revenue growth
5. Unique customers
6. Repeat customer rate
7. Purchase frequency
8. Delivery delay rate
9. Average review score
10. Freight-to-item ratio

## 5. Research Hypotheses

H1: Delivery delays are negatively associated with customer review scores.

H2: Freight-to-item-price ratios are associated with order values.

H3: Repeat customers have higher cumulative historical spending than one-time customers.

H4: Average order value differs across geographic regions.

## 6. Expected Business Outputs

- Sales performance assessment
- Customer segmentation findings
- Delivery performance assessment
- Statistical evidence and limitations
- Revenue scenario analysis
- Management recommendations

## 7. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when:

- All published KPIs have documented definitions.
- Calculations are validated across analytical tools.
- SQL queries and analytical scripts are reproducible.
- Statistical conclusions distinguish association from causation.
- Tableau dashboards accurately represent validated results.
- Business recommendations are supported by quantitative evidence.
